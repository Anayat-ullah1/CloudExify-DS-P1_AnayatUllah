1. Project Objective
The objective of this project was to analyze a retail sales dataset using Python. The analysis involved data loading, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualization, and generating business insights to support data-driven decision-making.
2. Dataset Overview
The dataset contains sales records from a retail superstore operating across different regions of the United States.
Dataset Summary
* Total Records: 9,800
* Total Features: 18
* Date Range: 03 January 2015 Ð 30 December 2018
* Number of Regions: 4
* Number of Categories: 3
* Number of Customers: 793
* Number of Cities: 529
3. Data Cleaning
The following preprocessing steps were performed before analysis:
* Loaded the dataset successfully using Pandas.
* Checked for missing values.
* Identified 11 missing values in the Postal Code column.
* Verified that there were no duplicate records.
* Converted the Order Date and Ship Date columns into DateTime format.
* Renamed the required columns to match the project requirements.
* Added a Units column for analysis compatibility.
The dataset was successfully prepared for exploratory analysis.

8. Monthly Sales Trend
Monthly sales analysis revealed fluctuations throughout the four-year period.
* Best Sales Month: November 2018
* Revenue in Best Month: $117,938.15
This indicates increased customer purchasing activity during the end-of-year period.

9. Key Business Insights
* The company generated a total revenue of $2.26 million during the analysis period.
* Technology was the most profitable product category, generating over $827,000 in revenue.
* Office Supplies recorded the highest number of customer orders but produced the lowest average sale value.
* The West region achieved the highest overall sales, followed by the East region.
* November 2018 was the highest-performing sales month, suggesting strong seasonal demand.
* A small number of premium products contributed a significant portion of the company's total revenue.
* Sales values were highly variable, indicating the presence of both low-cost and high-value transactions.
10. Conclusion
This project successfully demonstrated the complete sales analysis process using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib. The dataset was cleaned, explored, analyzed, and visualized to extract meaningful business insights.
The findings indicate that Technology products and the West region are the primary contributors to revenue, while seasonal trends suggest increased sales toward the end of the year. These insights can help management improve inventory planning, marketing strategies, and business decision-making.
Overall, the project achieved all of its objectives and demonstrates the effective use of data analysis techniques for extracting valuable information from sales data.
